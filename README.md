*WANJIKU'S BUSINESS PROBLEM

Wanjiku owns a small vegetable retail business selling products such as tomatoes, onions, sukuma, potatoes and avocados. She faces challenges in monitoring daily sales performance, managing inventory, identifying profitable products and understanding sales trends. Without proper analysis of her business data, making informed decisions about stock replenishment, pricing and business operations can be difficult. This project aims to analyze her sales and inventory data using Python to identify business patterns, evaluate product profitability, highlight operational challenges and provide data-driven recommendations that can help improve business performance and decision-making.
